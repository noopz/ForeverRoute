3. you also grant noopz a perpetual, worldwide, irrevocable, royalty-free license to use, change, sublicense and
   relicense your contribution under any terms, including commercial ones, so the project can change its licensing
   later without having to track down every contributor.

You keep the copyright in your contribution and you'll be credited for it. If you can't agree to this, please open an
issue describing the change instead.
